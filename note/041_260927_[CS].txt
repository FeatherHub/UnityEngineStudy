Identity

the fact that something is one and the same across time

---

The goblin you hit a second ago is the goblin you're looking at now
even though its position and health have changed

The morning star and the evening star turned out to be one planet

---

Identity is not a token
You can't store it, send it, or compare it directly

Everything else on the list is a stand-in that tries to track it
and each stand-in fails in its own way

identity is the target, the rest are proxies
and each proxy suits a particular kind of gap between you and the thing
